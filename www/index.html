Pick a vehicle:

Remote control – two sticks

🎮 Controls (tap “Controls” to switch): One stick (default) – the left stick is fully analog: push it diagonally to steer and accelerate at the same time; the right stick does the same, so use whichever thumb you like (flyers hold their height automatically; JUMP = hop). Point & go – push the left stick where you want to go and the vehicle turns that way. Classic RC – the original transmitter layout below.

🏎️🏍️🛴 Ground (race car, motorcycle, scooter), Classic RC: Left stick up = throttle, down = brake/reverse. Right stick left/right = steering. Hard steer at speed = drift. JUMP button hops over small obstacles and barriers.

🚁 Drone / Helicopter, Classic RC: Left stick up/down = altitude (center = hold). Right stick: left/right = yaw, up/down = forward/back. The helicopter tilts its rotor first, carries more momentum and hovers less steadily. JUMP = quick hop up.

🎁 Weapons: Drive through a glowing ? box – the weapon slot (bottom left, next to the left stick) rolls a random weapon. Tap the slot to fire it (or press F). Hits make racers spin/tumble briefly. A sticky bomb can be passed on by bumping another racer!

🛟 Flight assist: Leave the left stick centred and the drone/helicopter holds a safe height by itself – it follows the hills and ducks under gates, tables and the tunnel. Not steering? The nose gently lines up with the track. Drone and helicopter fly through glowing orange/cyan turbo rings instead – the assist lines you up with them.

🚀 Turbo pads: 15 orange chevron pads along the track – before jumps, ramps and the tunnel, on the straights, and in every slow grass / mud / puddle stretch. Drive over one for a short, strong speed boost. Flyers: a floating turbo ring hangs above every pad – fly through it for the same boost. 💣 Bombs are lobbed behind you. 🌪️ Over grass, mud and puddles flyers meet swirling wind gusts that slow them just like the ground slows cars (pale-blue on the minimap). All vehicles have the same speed: win with RC parts crate prizes and turbo pads!

⌨️ Keyboard: W/S or ↑/↓ = throttle / forward-back · A/D or ←/→ = steer (combine, e.g. W+A, for diagonals) · Space = jump (ground) / up (flyers) · E up · Shift/Q down · F = fire · R = reset to track · Esc = pause
