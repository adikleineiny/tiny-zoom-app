Remote control – two sticks

🏎️🏍️🛴 Ground (race car, motorcycle, scooter): Left stick up = throttle, down = brake/reverse. Right stick left/right = steering. Hard steer at speed = drift. JUMP button hops over small obstacles and barriers.

🚁 Drone / Helicopter: Left stick up/down = altitude (center = hold). Right stick: left/right = yaw, up/down = forward/back. The helicopter tilts its rotor first, carries more momentum and hovers less steadily. JUMP = quick hop up.

🎁 Weapons: Drive through a glowing ? box – the weapon slot (bottom left, next to the left stick) rolls a random weapon. Tap the slot to fire it (or press F). Hits make racers spin/tumble briefly. A sticky bomb can be passed on by bumping another racer!

🛟 Flight assist: Leave the left stick centred and the drone/helicopter holds a safe height by itself – it follows the hills and ducks under gates, tables and the tunnel. Not steering? The nose gently lines up with the track. Over a ⚡ pad it lands to charge.

⚡ Battery: Flyers drain fast. Land low over a blue charging pad to recharge (5 along the track). Low battery = less speed.

⌨️ Keyboard: W/S or ↑/↓ = throttle / forward-back · A/D or ←/→ = steer · Space = jump (ground) / up (flyers) · E up · Shift/Q down · F = fire · R = reset to track · Esc = pause
